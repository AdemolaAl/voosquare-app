1.11 **RevShare**: a percentage of Net Revenue.
1.12 **CPA**: a fixed amount per Qualified Customer on an approved deal.
1.13 **Hybrid**: a CPA amount plus a RevShare percentage on an approved deal.
1.14 **Hold**: a period an earning waits before it can be withdrawn. By default there is no Hold: earnings are Available as soon as they are earned (section 6).

1.16 **Refund Rate**: refunded (VooSquare orders refunded by us) and charged-back customers divided by New Paying Customers over the measurement window in section 5.
