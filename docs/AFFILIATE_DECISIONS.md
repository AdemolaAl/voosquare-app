## Access
- Public site at affiliate.voosquare.com (same Node app; host-based routing) and also reachable at voosquare.com/affiliates. "Affiliates" in the main site menu and member app. Visible **Log in** for existing affiliates (Voo ID login) and **Join** on the public site.
- Anyone with a Voo ID can create an affiliate account and see the full dashboard straight away; **earning starts only after approval**.

## Earning rules
- Commission is earned **only on purchases**. Top-ups into a tool wallet (e.g. Joinvoo, Vooads credits) do **not** count when deposited; they count when **used/spent** (tools send `spend` when credits are consumed). ~~Every commission sits on hold for 30 days~~ **Superseded 2026-10-05, see "Owner update" below: commissions are instant; used credits are non-refundable; only chargebacks and proven fraud reverse.**
- **CPA only for unique first-time paying customers**: a customer whose Voo ID existed before the affiliate click, or who is the same person as an existing customer (email incl. dots/+aliases, phone, device fingerprint, IP/subnet cluster, payment instrument, Telegram ID, name similarity, linked accounts), earns nothing. Automatic checks on every conversion plus a **one-tap Duplicate check** for fraud-check managers with verdict (Unique / Possible duplicate / Existing customer) and one-tap Approve / Decline-with-reason. Each CPA conversion approved or declined by admin or CPA fraud-check managers.
- CPA by approval, up to $300 (Replyvoo top plan), on qualified customers.
- RevShare tiers (2-month average of qualified new customers/month): Starter 0–9 20% · Pro 10–29 25% · Elite 30–74 30% · Master 75–149 35% · Partner 150–249 40% · Senior Partner 250–399 45% · Legend 400+ 50% (approval). Product caps (hard-locked, see the owner update below): Vooads and Replyvoo 35%, others 50%, Inner Circle 0%, Gatevoo 0% unless enabled. Affleego is not part of the program. New rates apply to payments after the change.
- **Lifetime RevShare health condition (new):**
  - Earnings activate after the first **5 qualified customers** (qualified at their first payment since 2026-10-05) (commissions before that are tracked as "waiting for activation" and released once 5 is reached).
  - The first **90 days** after activation are a ramp-up period.
  - After that, to keep lifetime RevShare **Active**, an affiliate needs at least **50 new qualified customers in the last rolling 90 days** (admin-configurable; owner may lower it, e.g. to 30).
  - Health states: **Active** (≥ threshold), **At risk** (projected to fall below within 14 days or below 70% of pace), **Grace** (below threshold: 30 days to recover, still earning), **Paused** (after grace: RevShare on existing customers stops accruing — no back pay — until back at threshold; new-customer CPA deals unaffected unless admin sets otherwise).
  - Health is shown on the dashboard with a live meter, days left, and what's needed. Admin can exempt an affiliate.
- **Sub-affiliate program:** affiliates can recruit sub-affiliates and earn **2.5%** of their subs' approved commissions (paid by Zedapex, never deducted from the sub). **5%** when they have **50 active subs who each have ≥10 qualified paid customers**. Referrer must be approved and Active. One level only. Sub network reporting (subs list, their customers, earnings, status).
- Reporting includes **redeposit / repeat purchase stats** (repeat payments per customer, rebill rate, revenue per customer over time, cohorts).

## Payouts
- Minimum **$50** (affiliate program). Crypto: USDT (TRC20) and BTC; paid within **1–3 business days** (faster by tier per spec). Four-eyes approval above $2,000.
- **Receipts:** every payout has a downloadable **PDF receipt** with the VooSquare logo, receipt number, affiliate details, period, line items (commissions by product, adjustments, clawbacks), amount, network, wallet, tx hash, date; receipts history page; statements per month.
- **External APIs:** KYC provider adapter (generic: e.g. Smile ID, Sumsub, Veriff via API key + webhook; manual review fallback); payout provider adapter (manual mark-paid with tx hash by default; optional API provider configured in admin, e.g. NOWPayments / exchange payouts / Gatevoo later). KYC required before first payout (configurable threshold).

## Owner update 2026-10-05: instant RevShare, used credits are final
Owner: "30 days is a long wait, many would get tired. Rev share should be automatic. If someone uses a credit, it's non refundable." This overrides the hold, qualification and refund rules above and in AFFILIATE_SPEC.md.
- **RevShare is automatic and instant.** Commission on `spend` (credits used) and on purchases goes straight to **Available**: no hold, no manual approval. Sub-affiliate overrides on it are instant too. "Waiting for activation" (before 5 qualified customers) stays.
- **Holds are admin settings, all 0 by default** (Program settings → Earnings timing; `settings.holds`): credits used (`spend_days`), VooSquare orders paid with Gatevoo/crypto/manual (`order_days`), VooSquare orders paid by card through Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe or a custom API (`card_order_days`, chargeback-capable), CPA after approval (`cpa_days`, `cpa_long_days` for ≥ `cpa_long_cents`, `cpa_new_days`). A product's own hold can only lengthen it. A hold applies to earnings created after it is saved; the hourly job releases them. The dashboard shows "On hold" only when it is above zero.
- **Credits used are non-refundable.** A tool `refund` never claws back commission (it is logged as `refund_final`, "Refund by the tool (commission final)", and does not count toward refund rate, contests or qualification). Unused wallet top-ups never earned commission. An admin refund of a **VooSquare order** reverses that order's commission.
- **Chargebacks still claw back** (card disputes), including an approved CPA within 90 days of qualifying: negative balance carried forward and deducted from the next payout.
- **Qualification without waiting:** a customer is qualified at their **first** counted payment (no second payment, no 30-day wait). Activation at 5, tiers, fast lane, health (50 in rolling 90 days), contests and sub-affiliate counts use it immediately. A product can still be set to "Second payment" in Deals and products.
- **Contests:** scores are final when the contest ends (`qual_days` default 0, was 45); the two-person finalise/sign-off stays.
- **CPA:** keeps the fraud/duplicate review with one-tap approve/decline; once approved it is Available (CPA hold setting default 0, admin can set one). "Need more time" now only records extra review time.
- **Fraud safety net:** a conversion later proven to be fraud is reversed through a **fraud reversal** (proposed by fraud check or admin with a reason, approved by a different finance user; reason code `fraud`; both steps in the audit log; works on available and recently paid earnings). Four-eyes on payouts over $2,000 is unchanged.
- **Upgrade:** on first boot after this change, stored settings still equal to the old defaults (30/60-day holds, "second payment or 30 days", 45-day contest windows on unfinished contests) move to the new ones once (`aff_policy_v` = 2); deliberate custom values are kept. Commissions already on hold keep their dates and are released by the hourly job; unqualified customers with a counted payment are qualified by the hourly catch-up.
- Terms: section 1.8, 1.9, 4.4, 6, 7 and 13.2 of AFFILIATE_TERMS.md updated, including: "Credits that have been used are non-refundable; commission on used credits is final except in the case of a payment chargeback or proven fraud."

## Owner update 2026-10-05 (2): Affleego out of the program, hard caps, adjustment controls
Owner: "Affleego operates separately. We're not selling anything there. No commission for that. Remove it from anything commission. It operates independently, but still part of our product." and "35% max on heavy ones." This overrides anything above and in AFFILIATE_SPEC.md that lists Affleego as an offer.
- **Affleego is not part of the affiliate program.** It is not an offer, deal, CPA product, tier/cap row, deep-link or link-builder destination, creative product, contest product, report filter or postback product, and it is not in the public site, FAQ, terms or emails of the program. Affleego stays a VooSquare product everywhere else (tools page, Voo ID sign-in, VooSquare Academy mentions, welcome emails).
- **Enforced in code, not by a setting** (`src/aff/core.js` `NOT_IN_PROGRAM`): any `spend`, `refund`, `chargeback`, `plan_*` or `wallet_topup` event or order for product `affleego` is ignored by the affiliate engine. It earns nothing and creates no conversion, customer, qualification, activation, tier, health or contest effect. An Affleego tracking link (old saved links, `?p=affleego`) lands on VooSquare, and `affleego.com` is not an approved deep-link domain. The API refuses Affleego for deals, deal requests, links, coupons, creatives and postbacks (it is an unknown product there).
- **Existing installs** (migration `004`): the `aff_products.affleego` rule is switched off (not commissionable, cap 0, no CPA). Active or paused Affleego deals end, pending requests are declined, scheduled Affleego offer changes are cancelled, Affleego coupons and creatives are deactivated, saved Affleego links are archived, and Affleego is removed from contest and postback product lists. Nothing is deleted: commissions, conversions and the audit log keep their history (customers already qualified before this change stay qualified).
- **Hard RevShare caps:** Vooads and Replyvoo at most **35%**, every other product at most **50%**. The API refuses a higher `cap_pct` (400) and a Hybrid RevShare above the hard cap, even for the owner; the back office limits the inputs. The engine never pays tier RevShare above the hard cap whatever is stored (per-affiliate deals by the owner or a program super admin are documented in the back-office admin docs).
- **Manual balance adjustments** (finance): a **reason is always required** (the affiliate sees it). A **positive adjustment above $500** (`settings.adjustments.four_eyes_cents`) is not paid at once: it waits for a **second finance or admin user** (never the person who asked, never the affiliate themselves) in Payouts → "Adjustments waiting for a second approval" and on the affiliate's page. Every request carries an **idempotency key** (a retry returns the first result), and the same affiliate + amount + reason again within **60 seconds** is refused as a double click (409). Deductions and adjustments up to $500 apply at once, as before.
- **Security:** a product's `support_webhook` (support replies sent to a tool) now goes through the same SSRF guard as postbacks (`src/netguard.js`: public hosts only, resolved IP checked, no redirects, 5 s timeout); a private address is refused when the product is saved.

## Owner update 2026-10-05 (3): answers "yes" — tool refunds on plans, super admins, founder quote, terms v1.1
- **Tool refunds never reverse commission, plans included.** A `refund` event from a tool is final for the affiliate whatever it refunds: used credits, a plan payment or renewal, an overage or a one-time purchase. It is logged as `refund_final` (label "Refund by the tool (commission final)", `plan` recorded when known) and does not touch the refund rate, qualification, CPA or contests (`src/aff/engine.js` `processReversal` → `recordFinalRefund`; tested with a plan payment refund with and without `original_event_id`). **Still reversing:** chargebacks, admin refunds of VooSquare orders (Premium, paid rooms) and approved fraud reversals.
- **Custom RevShare deals (up to 90%, "Special deal" tag above 50%): owner and program super admins.** The `aff_admin` role may now create and change them (`createRevshareDeal` / `changeRevshareDeal` check `roles().admin`); managers, finance and fraud check get 403. The Special-deal confirm step, the AI-tools warning, the audit log (actor recorded) and privacy (that affiliate and staff only) are unchanged.
- **Founder quote** on the affiliate site is approved as is. No "draft" tag remains in `public/js/aff-*.js` or `public/affiliate.html` (the terms quote style class was renamed `t-note`; a test guards it).
- **Terms v1.1 (2026-10-05), re-acceptance.** `src/aff/core.js` `TERMS` sets the minimum version (a stored older `terms_version` in settings can never pin v1.0; admins may set a newer one, never a lower one). Affiliates whose last accepted version is older see a banner on every page and a "We've updated the affiliate terms — what changed" pop-up (once a session) with the summary from the terms' "Changes in v1.1" section, a link to the full terms and **Accept**. **Blocked until accepted: payout requests only** (`POST /api/aff/payouts` → 409 `{ terms_required, terms_version }`, `blocked_by: ['terms_update']`). Earning, links, reports, postbacks, the stats API and the dashboard are never blocked, so nobody loses money. Every acceptance is stored with version, time and source in `aff_terms_acceptances` (migration `006`, backfilled from existing acceptances) and in the audit log (`terms.accept`). New applicants accept v1.1 directly at step 4.
