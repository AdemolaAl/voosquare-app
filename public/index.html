Questions

Good questions.

Is VooSquare really free?

Yes. Your Voo ID, the free community rooms, the free Academy courses and the blog are free for life. Each tool has its own free way to start, and you only pay inside a tool when you use it.

Do I need a separate account for each tool?

No. Your Voo ID opens every tool. Sign in once on VooSquare and launch every Zedapex tool from your dashboard.

Is the community free?

Yes. Every account gets the free rooms. Premium adds the premium rooms, and a few small paid rooms are sold separately.

What does Premium include?

Premium rooms, every Academy course, a monthly live campaign breakdown with Dchessking, templates and playbooks, priority support and early access to new tools.

Who teaches in the Academy?

Dchessking, founder of Zedapex, and the media buyers on the Zedapex team. Every lesson comes from campaigns we ran with our own money.

Is it only for media buyers?

It started with media buyers, because that's who we are. It's built for anyone running performance at scale: affiliate teams, agencies, Telegram operators and brands that sell online. Follow the law and the ad platform's rules in every country you run.
